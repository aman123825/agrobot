# Pilot Stage 0 Toolkit — ready-to-use (interviews, due-diligence, route walk, record template)

**04-Aug-2026 | executes the ₹0 gates in `GRAPE_EVIDENCE_PILOT_PLAN.md` §2**

> These are the actual field/desk tools for Stage 0. None needs hardware or capital. Run them first; a red result here kills the venture cheaply before any build.

## A. Exporter / pack-house interview script (30 min, in person or phone)
Goal: does a solvent, audit-obligated buyer **pay** for a GrapeNet-ready evidence + PHI record? Ask, then shut up and listen.

1. Walk me through how you compile your **GrapeNet / Residue Monitoring Plan** records today — who does it, how long does it take?
2. In the last 2 seasons, have you had (or narrowly avoided) a **residue rejection** or an audit query? What did it cost you?
3. Who is liable in your chain when a consignment is rejected — you, the pack-house, or the farm?
4. How do you currently verify a grower actually respected the **pre-harvest interval** before you buy?
5. If you had a per-plot, geotagged, agronomist-signed treatment+PHI record that dropped straight into your GrapeNet file, what would that be worth to you **per acre or per season**? (Get a number.)
6. Would you pay for it as a **service**, or expect it bundled into the grower price?
7. How many grower plots do you source from, and how tightly clustered are they? (Route density.)
8. What would make you **not** trust or use such a record?
9. Who else already offers you something like this (agronomist, Fyllo, in-house)? What do you pay them?
10. If we ran a free 8–12 plot pilot this season, would you commit QA time to review the records?

**Green flags:** names a ₹ figure; has a recent rejection scare; current record-keeping is painful. **Red flags:** "our agronomist already does this free"; won't pay a separate line; plots are scattered.

## B. Buyer/FPO due-diligence checklist (desk, ~1 day)
Confirm the payer can actually fund a season (adoption dossier disqualified JTFPC at ~₹1.19 L FY22).
- [ ] Entity type + registration (MCA / registrar).
- [ ] **Audited turnover** last 2–3 years (MCA filings / annual report).
- [ ] Net worth / reserves (positive?).
- [ ] Existing machinery/CHC assets and any grant history (SFAC/NABARD/SMAM).
- [ ] Export volume & destinations (APEDA data if available).
- [ ] Number of grower members / supplier plots and their geographic spread.
- [ ] Existing tech spend (do they already pay Fyllo/agronomist? how much?).
**Pass:** turnover comfortably covers a paid season; positive reserves; concentrated supplier base.

## C. Route-density GPS-walk protocol (½ day per cluster)
The single biggest cost driver (rover ≈ 4–6 ac/day).
1. Pick one candidate grower cluster the buyer sources from.
2. Log GPS for each plot boundary (phone app is fine); record acreage per plot.
3. Measure **inter-plot travel** (road distance/time between plots on the route).
4. Compute **plots/km²** and **acres reachable per working day** at ~0.6–1.0 m/s in-row + transit.
5. Derive **₹/acre-pass** = (day cost: operator + transport + amortised rover) ÷ acres/day.
**Pass:** ₹/acre-pass is **below** the buyer's willingness (from A5). If not, the wedge is dead at this cluster — try a denser one or stop.

## E. Go / no-go after Stage 0
- **All four Stage-0 gates green** → proceed to Stage 1 (build + agronomist partner).
- **Payer or route-density red** → stop or re-scope to a different buyer/cluster. Do **not** start a build.
- Cost so far to reach this decision: **≈ interviews + a day of filings + a GPS walk.**
