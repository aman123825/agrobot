# AgriRover — Legal Questions for Counsel (PARKED)

**STATUS: pre-pilot gate — NOT needed for the problem-research or prototype-build phase.**

Open this only when approaching one of these triggers:
1. You are about to **charge** for a treatment recommendation, or deliver a signed/timestamped record someone **acts on**.
2. You are about to run a **paid pilot** with a real FPO / exporter / pack-house.
3. You are about to **apply chemicals** in a client's field (vs. bench / research only).

Until then: keep the bot's value in **sensing and evidence** (scouting, counts, maps, ripeness %, soil data, geotagged records) — measuring a field needs no licence — and keep any **treatment recommendation / prescription** as a partnered agronomist's signed output, not the company's. That single design choice means no future legal answer can invalidate what you build.

> This is a question list to put to qualified counsel — an Indian **agri-food / pesticide-regulatory + product-liability** advocate (Maharashtra-admitted a plus). It is not legal advice.

## Who answers what (spend money only on the load-bearing parts)

| Section | Best answerer | Cost |

## A. Grower liability & the FSS Act exemption (tests `[G03]`)
1. Under the FSS Act 2006, is a farmer selling his own primary produce liable for a pesticide-residue (MRL) breach on it? Cite the provision/exemption.
2. Does "primary production" sit outside the FSSA "food business operator" definition, and where does liability begin in the chain (aggregator, commission agent, trader, pack-house, processor, exporter)?
3. If a grower is not liable, is there any residual civil/contractual exposure (buyer contracts, APMC rules, warranty) a residue breach could still trigger?
4. Does the exemption change if he sells via an FPO, to an exporter, or under contract farming?

## B. Who may legally give paid plant-protection advice / prescriptions
5. Is giving paid pesticide recommendations a regulated activity (Insecticides Act 1968 or otherwise)?
6. What qualification/licence must the person/company signing a recommendation hold?
7. Can a private company issue a paid treatment recommendation, or must a licensed individual sign it?
8. How does the pending Pesticide Management Bill change this — what should we design for now?

## C. Label-lock and recommendation liability (tests `[G23]`)
9. Are recommendations legally confined to CIB&RC-registered label claims (crop, dose, interval, max sprays, PHI)? Exposure for off-label but common uses?
10. Liability of the agronomist and of the company for a signed recommendation that causes: (a) crop damage, (b) an MRL breach, (c) a failed export consignment, (d) applicator injury?
11. Does signing/timestamping increase attributable liability vs informal advice? Verbal vs written?
12. Can liability be limited/disclaimed by contract/T&Cs, and how enforceable is that (incl. Consumer Protection Act 2019)?

## D. The "signed record / audit artefact" product
13. Legal weight of a private, timestamped, geotagged treatment-and-PHI record — admissible/relied-on in an APEDA/FSSAI/export dispute, or purely internal?
14. If our record is later shown wrong (missed residue, wrong PHI), what is our liability to grower, buyer, or a third party who relied on it?
15. What disclaimers/sign-offs/accreditation make the record credible to a pack-house/exporter without open-ended liability for us?

## E. PHI, MRL & chain-of-liability enforcement
16. Is the pre-harvest interval legally enforceable, and against whom? What records must a seller/exporter keep?
17. On an MRL breach at market or port, how is liability apportioned grower → aggregator → exporter? Who pays?
18. Any mandated residue-testing/documentation duties in the domestic tomato chain (vs export)?

## F. Export compliance — where the real liability sits (tests persona D)
19. For grape/vegetable exports, who is legally responsible for a residue rejection — exporter, pack-house, or registered farm?
20. Binding traceability/record obligations under APEDA (GrapeNet, residue monitoring plan, farm registration)? Would a per-plot rover record satisfy/complement them?
21. Legal/financial consequences to an exporter of a border rejection (suspension ladder, de-listing) — i.e., how big is the liability we'd help manage?

## G. Insurance & risk transfer
22. Is professional-indemnity / E&O cover for an agronomy advisory available in India, and roughly what cost?
23. Is such cover a practical prerequisite before we sign recommendations? Which insurers underwrite agri-advisory liability?
24. Can liability be transferred to a partnered licensed agronomist/agency instead of held by us?

## H. Structure, registration & contracts
25. Does the paid service need a specific business registration, agri-advisory licence, or state Agriculture Department empanelment?
26. Any APMC / Maharashtra state-law constraints on paid services tied to produce marketing?
27. What contract structure (with grower, FPO, exporter) best isolates liability and clarifies ownership of the recommendation and the data?

## I. Data & privacy
28. If we collect farm location, ownership and practice data, do DPDP Act 2023 obligations apply, and what consent/handling is required?
29. Any restrictions on storing/sharing geotagged farm and treatment data with FPOs, exporters, or KVKs?

## The one rule
`[G03]` (used to *exclude* the grower pitch) and `[G23]` / section D (whether *we* carry liability) must come as a **written opinion from a qualified lawyer**, not a helpdesk or a mentor's verbal take. Everything else can start free. None of it is needed until a trigger above is hit.
