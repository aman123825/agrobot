## 0. What "done" looks like for Level 1
A credible Level-1 customer-discovery result = **all four** of:
1. **~40–50 real conversations** logged across segments (see §8 targets).
2. Each hypothesis in §2 marked **validated / invalidated / unclear** with quotes + numbers.
3. At least one **advancement/commitment** captured (a warm intro, a pilot-slot offer, a signed intent, or a ₹ figure) — not just compliments.
4. A written **go / pivot / no-go** decision tied to the pilot gates.

## 1. The 7 golden rules (read before EVERY interview — from *The Mom Test*)
1. Talk about **their life and past**, not your idea.
2. **Don't pitch, don't demo first.** Your idea should not come up for the first 10 minutes.
3. **Never ask "would you buy / would you use it?"** — hypotheticals lie. Ask what they did last season.
4. Ask for **specific, recent stories**: "Tell me about the last time…".
5. Chase the **cost of the problem** — in ₹, in lost consignments, in hours, in loans.
6. Seek a **commitment or advancement** (time / reputation / money), not praise. "That's cool" is a warning sign.
7. **Shut up and listen** — you should talk <30% of the time. Record (with consent) or take a scribe.

**Anti-patterns that will waste your ₹75,000:** pitching then asking if they like it; leading questions ("don't you hate residue rejections?"); accepting compliments as validation; talking to whoever is easy instead of the real payer; only hearing what confirms the idea.

## 4. Interview scripts (Mom-Test style — adapt, don't read robotically)

### A. KVK scientist / agronomist (Baramati CoE, Narayangaon)
1. What are the **top 3 problems** growers in this cluster bring to you each season?
2. Walk me through what a grower **actually does** when they suspect a disease or pest — who do they call first?
3. How do growers here decide **when to spray** — calendar, or scouting counts?
4. For export crops, how do growers/exporters handle **residue and PHI compliance** today? Where does it break?
5. Which crop's problems are **most economically damaging** here right now, and why?
6. Has any **scouting/monitoring tech** (drones, Fyllo sensors, robots) been tried here? What happened — did it stick? Why/why not?
7. If a service produced weekly **scouting evidence + agronomist-reviewed PHI records**, who here would find it valuable — and who would **pay**?
8. *(Commitment)* Would KVK be willing to **co-validate a small field trial**? Who are the serious FPOs/exporters you'd **introduce** us to?

### B. Exporter / pack-house (the likely payer)
1. Walk me through how you compile your **GrapeNet / Residue Monitoring Plan** records today — who does it, how long?
2. In the last 2 seasons, any **residue rejection or audit query** (or a near miss)? What did it cost?
3. When a consignment is rejected, **who is liable** — you, the pack-house, or the farm?
4. How do you **verify** a grower respected the PHI before you buy?
5. If a per-plot, geotagged, agronomist-signed treatment+PHI record dropped straight into your GrapeNet file — **what's that worth to you, per acre or per season?** (get a number)
6. Would you pay for it as a **service**, or expect it bundled into the grower price?
7. How many grower plots do you source from, and **how clustered** are they?
8. What would make you **not trust or use** such a record?
9. Who offers you something like this now (agronomist, Fyllo, in-house)? **What do you pay them?**
10. *(Commitment)* If we ran a **free 8–12 plot pilot** this season, would you commit QA time to review the records?

### C. FPO / producer-company manager
1. How many member plots do you aggregate, and how **geographically tight** are they?
2. What services do you run today (CHC, grading, inputs)? What do members **complain about most**?
3. Do you compile any **compliance / spray records** for members or buyers? How painful is it?
4. Would a **cluster scouting + evidence service** help member retention, grade, or price?
5. Would the **FPO pay**, or pass the cost to the buyer?
6. *(Diligence)* What was your **audited turnover** last year — could you fund a season's service? *(be tactful)*
7. *(Commitment)* Would you introduce us to a buyer you sell to?

### D. Grower (grape / tomato / pomegranate)
1. Tell me about your **last bad season** — what went wrong, what did it cost you in ₹?
2. Walk me through your **spray decisions last season** — how many sprays, what did they cost?
3. Who do you **trust for advice** — the dealer, KVK, a neighbour, an app?
4. Have you ever **lost a sale or got a lower price** over quality or residue?
5. Have you ever **paid for any farm advice or service**? How much, and did you continue? *(past behaviour — the real WTP signal)*
6. Would you let a **machine drive your rows** and photograph plants? Any concern?
7. What do you **wish you'd known earlier** each season?

### E. Input dealer (incumbent competitor)
1. How do farmers here decide what to **buy** from you?
2. Do you **advise on spray timing / product choice**? Do they pay for that advice or is it free with the sale?
3. What share of your customers **follow your advice** vs do their own thing?
*(Purpose: understand the free-advice channel that competes with any paid advisory.)*

## 5. What to show — and when
- Do **not** open with the bot. Learn first (rules §1).
- Only in the **last 5 minutes**, show a **one-page mockup of the evidence record** (from `PILOT_STAGE0_TOOLKIT.md §D`) and ask "what's wrong with this / what's missing / who would use it?" — to get reactions, not applause.
- Carry an **honest** message: AgriRover has **no field results yet**; you are here to learn. Do not promise savings or performance (`README` status; keeps you credible and legally safe).

## 6. Data capture (fill one per conversation)
`Date · Name · Role · Segment · Location · Crop · [problem confirmed? cost in ₹] · [who they said pays] · [WTP number, if any] · [current alternative & its cost] · [commitment obtained: none/time/intro/₹] · top 3 quotes · my hypothesis updates`

**Signals scoreboard (update weekly):** for each hypothesis H1–H8 tally validated / invalidated / unclear with the count of interviews behind it.

**Commitment ladder (the real currency of discovery):**
- *Time* — agrees to a follow-up / gives an hour.
- *Reputation* — makes an intro, or gives a quote you may cite.
- *Money* — signs a letter of intent, or pre-commits to pay for a pilot.
Record which each conversation produced. **Zero commitments across many chats = the idea is weak, however polite people were.**

## 9. Synthesis & decision (the Level-1 deliverable)
After ~40 conversations, write a 2–3 page memo:
- Each hypothesis H1–H8: **validated / invalidated / unclear** + the numbers and quotes behind it.
- Who the **real paying customer** turned out to be (or that none emerged).
- The **WTP number** you actually heard (compare to the ₹800–950/acre-pass a deep scan needs, and the ₹14.89/acre grower-info benchmark).
- **Decision:** proceed to the `GRAPE_EVIDENCE_PILOT_PLAN.md` Stage-1 build / **pivot** the customer or value-prop / **stop**.
- Any **commitments** secured (intros, pilot slots, LOIs) — these are your Level-2 ammunition.

## 10. Honest guardrails
- **No performance claims.** You have no field data — say so. Curiosity earns more than a pitch.
- **Don't promise a paid advisory** or prescribe chemicals — keep an agronomist as the signer and see `LEGAL_QUESTIONS.md` before any money changes hands.
- **Fight confirmation bias:** actively hunt for the conversation that proves you wrong. The most valuable interview is the one that kills a hypothesis cheaply — that is exactly what the ₹75,000 is for.
