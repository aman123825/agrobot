Autonomous crop-scouting rover

AgriRover

A rugged, low-cost field rover that walks tomato rows for smallholder farms, captures geotagged crop evidence, runs on-device AI for weeds, disease and obstacles, and hands an agronomist a reviewable record — instead of asking a farmer to guess.

01

Problem & approach

Prototype rover on a close-row scouting pass. Tank drive, pan-tilt camera, GPS mast, rear utility deck.

Scouting does not scale by walking. Pest and disease outbreaks in tomato are caught late because nobody inspects every row every week.

Spray decisions are made blind. Without dated, located evidence, treatment turns into calendar spraying — cost, residue and pre-harvest-interval risk.

Nothing is written down. No image trail, no treatment log, no way to check whether an intervention actually worked.

Engineering that makes it survivable

Layered stop logic

  • VL53L1X ToF proximity cut-off
  • Obstacle inference veto on drive
  • IMU tilt and rollover threshold
  • Heartbeat watchdog on the link
  • Motor-stall current detection

Field robustness

  • Thermal guardian: CPU, pack, ambient
  • Fuel gauge + current monitor budgeting
  • Encoder + IMU odometry for row keeping
  • Simulation rig — full stack, no hardware
  • pytest suite over AI, nav, data, safety

Trust & security

  • HMAC-authenticated, encrypted commands
  • MQTT over TLS with broker auth
  • ESP32 secure-boot path, hardened Pi image
  • Immutable evidence and treatment records
  • Published threat model in SECURITY.md
